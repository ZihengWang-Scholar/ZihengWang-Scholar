## 👋 About Me

Hi! I'm **Ziheng Wang(王梓恒)**, an undergraduate student majoring in Computer Science and Technology at the Honors College, Northwestern Polytechnical University (NPU).

My research interests primarily focus on Large Video Understanding, and Visual Understanding
I am particularly interested in understanding and evaluating AI-generated visual content, with a focus on trustworthy video generation, entity consistency, and physical plausibility.

I am always open to academic discussions and research collaborations.
